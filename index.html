Utilities

Set these up first — ideally a week before move-in. Power and water can take 1–2 business days to activate.

Electricity
Florida Power & Light (FPL)
Serves all of Santa Rosa County
Call
Pro tip from a recent buyer
Skip the phone call — FPL is fully online.
  • Go to fpl.com
  • Enter your new address & move-in date
  • Create an account & verify ID
  • Confirmation arrives by email within minutes

Set the service start date to your closing date so power is on the day the home is yours.

Water — Holley Navarre (Most Navarre homes)
Holley Navarre Water System
Serves most Navarre homes incl. Holley by the Sea
Call
Holley Navarre Water — pro tip from a recent buyer
This provider has a 3-step process. Do them in order:
  1. Submit the new service application on their website: hnws-fl.com
  2. Email them a screenshot of the top section of your signed purchase contract (showing buyer names, address, and legal description — see example below) plus a photo of your driver's license
  3. Call (850) 939-2427 to confirm they received everything and schedule activation
Example: What to send Holley Navarre Water Just this section of your signed Contract for Residential Sale and Purchase — the part showing buyer name(s), property address, legal description, and Tax ID. The Brown team can email this snippet to you on closing day.

Garbage & recycling — pro tip from a recent buyer
Trash haulers only schedule about 2 weeks out.

Don’t call too early — they typically can’t set up service more than ~2 weeks before your start date. Wait until you’re inside that 2-week window, then call to schedule your first pickup and cart delivery.
